Start with 2-3 short opening paragraphs:
- what prompted the post
- the central question or claim
- why the topic matters

## Context

State the core idea clearly. Keep this section focused on the motivation and framing.

## Main Notes

Organize the article into 2-4 subsections. Each subsection should develop one idea, not multiple loosely related ones.

### Idea One

Explain the idea, then connect it to an example, observation, or implication.

### Idea Two

Continue with one clear thread. Prefer shorter paragraphs over dense blocks.

## Closing Notes

Close by summarizing the practical takeaway, broader implication, or open question.
