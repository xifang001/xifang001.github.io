- Proposed the pyramid-input and pyramid-output feature abstraction network (PIPO-FAN) to reduce semantic gaps across feature scales.
- Introduced a target-adaptive loss with a unified training strategy to enable segmentation over multiple partially labeled datasets using a single model.
- Released the codebase publicly at [DIAL-RPI/PIPO-FAN](https://github.com/DIAL-RPI/PIPO-FAN).
