- Built an AI-assisted fluoroscopy annotation and human-in-the-loop refinement pipeline that reduced per-video labeling time by 83%, from 12 hours to 2 hours.
- Trained and deployed an nnU-Net for metal artifact reduction using the resulting scalable dataset.
- Covered 11 C-arm systems and removed about 95% of metal-related artifacts, including artifacts from scopes and WFGs, while preserving underlying tissue structures.
